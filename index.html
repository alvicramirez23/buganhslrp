ABOUT THE PORTAL
BUGA NHS · LEARNING RESOURCE PORTAL

Learning resources, within reach.

A centralized directory of Buga NHS learning folders, organized by grade level, section, and subject area. It helps learners, parents, and teachers find the right Google Drive resources in just a few steps.

Why I developed this portal

I created this portal to make learning materials easier to locate and to support learning continuity when regular classes are disrupted. During HINAY (Ease-In) and other emergencies, it offers one organized starting point so learners can continue at a manageable pace and teachers can share resources more conveniently.

WELCOME TO YOUR LEARNING HUB

Your quick guide.

Find your class resources in just a few clicks. Here's how to get around the portal.

  1. 01

    Choose your grade & section

    Select Grade 7–12 from the grade shortcuts, then use Grade & section to narrow the results.

  2. 02

    Find your subject or teacher

    Choose a Subject area or try G9 math, AP, or a Subject Teacher's surname. Search shows matching folders as you type. On phones, tap Filters to browse categories.

  3. 03

    Open the learning folder

    Click Open folder to view materials in Google Drive. You may need to sign in or request access from the folder owner.

  4. 04

    Save folders for later

    Tap the bookmark icon on a resource, then open Saved in the header whenever you need it again.

Tip: Use Ctrl + K to search on a computer. Open this guide anytime using Guide in the top bar.
